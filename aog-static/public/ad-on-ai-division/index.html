FAQs

Questions, answered.

Do we need technical skills to start?

No. The program meets your team where they are and builds practical confidence from the ground up — no engineering required.

How is this different from a course we’d watch?

Most AI training gets watched once and forgotten. Ours is hands-on — every module ends in something your team actually builds and uses.

Is AI safe to use in our business?

Yes. We focus on responsible, secure adoption with clear guardrails suited to your business.

What happens after the program?

You keep lifetime academy access, a monthly trends webinar and the Implementation Lab as an optional ongoing add-on.

Who runs it?

Operators, not influencers — we built this inside a real, multi-country business, then packaged it for yours.
