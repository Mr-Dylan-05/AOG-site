FAQs

The questions we hear most.

How is this different from AI Enablement?
It’s shorter, and focused on getting Claude working for you day to day. You build each thing once. AI Enablement builds each one three times over, then goes on to AI agents.
What do I need to take part?
A paid Claude plan. The other AI tools in Module 5 are optional and paid for separately.
Can I cancel?
Yes, you can cancel your membership any time. That ends your one‑on‑one support, community and ongoing academy access.
How do I book my one-on-one support?
It comes with your membership. Book through the calendar link in your platform, in sessions of at least 1 hour.
Do unused support hours roll over?
No. The hour is use-it-or-lose-it each month. If you need more, extra support is $300 per hour, ex GST.
Is the platform secure?
Yes, all features and content are hosted securely.
How long can I use the Learning Academy?
Your program includes 4 months from your start date. With a membership, access continues for as long as you’re a member.
When do my entitlements begin?
From the day you start your course. If you’ve added a membership, your community access starts straight away too.
