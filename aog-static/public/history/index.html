History

Unveiling the Past, Shaping the Future

Since 2008

Resilient Since 2008: Growing, Adapting, and Innovating

Born in 2008 we’ve been through it all! Global financial crisis, pandemic, flooding, earthquakes, cyclones and fires! Nearly 20 years on we’ve kept growing and innovating, adapting to our customers needs and calls for help.

Trusted by over 20 000 Australian business customers, the Ad On Group is a trusted name for offshore staff via Ad On Workforce, digital marketing via Ad On Digital and advertising on hold messages via Ad On Hold.

Ad On Hold to Digital

Trusted Solutions

Our oldest division, Ad On Hold has long term, strategic partnerships with Telstra, with their DOT phone system having Ad on Hold as part of the offering. Telstra Business System ( their telephone systems division ) also have Ad on Hold embedded into their solution. If Telstra trust us, so should you!

Then came Ad On Digital, our digital marketing division which was developed in response to our customers requests. We are now one of the largest providers of packaged digital marketing solutions in the country and value our partnerships with Google and Meta ( Facebook ) enabling us to be a one stop marketing shop for our customers.

Ad On Workforce

Offshore Solutions for Onshore Success

With unemployment being at a record low, along came our largest and fastest growing division, Ad On Workforce! We’re helping our customers get the job done with our fantastic offshore staff based in South Africa and our Philippines offices. From admin and finance roles, to customer service and marketing and more, we’ve got you covered. Get the heavy lifting done offshore so you can focus on the high value aspects of your business!
