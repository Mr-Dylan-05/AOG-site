FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

AEO and AI search
What is AEO and how is it different from SEO?

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimisation. It involves improving your website so that it can be better understood and ranked by traditional search engines such as Google.

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimisation. It focuses on structuring and improving your website content so that AI-powered platforms and answer engines can better understand your business and the information you provide.

Both are important as the way people search for information, products and services continues to evolve.

Why does my business need AEO?

People are increasingly using AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini to ask questions, research services and compare businesses.

AEO helps make your website content clearer, more structured and easier for these platforms to interpret.

It is not a guarantee that an AI platform will mention or recommend your business, but it helps establish a stronger foundation for AI discovery.

Will my business appear in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?

No agency can guarantee that an AI platform will mention, recommend or refer customers to your business.

These platforms use their own systems and processes to select and present information.

Our services are designed to improve your website's structure, content and online information so that your business has a stronger foundation for discovery.

Do you guarantee Google rankings or AI recommendations?

No. Search engine rankings and AI-generated responses are controlled by independent third-party platforms and can change without notice.

We cannot guarantee specific rankings, website traffic, leads, sales, AI mentions or recommendations.

Our services focus on improving your website, content and online foundations to support better visibility.
